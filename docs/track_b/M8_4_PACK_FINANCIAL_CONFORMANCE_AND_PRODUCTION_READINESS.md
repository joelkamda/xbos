# M8.4 Pack Financial Conformance and Production-Readiness Evidence

M8.4 adds a schema-neutral, reusable proof boundary for industry and tenant packs. It does not add financial authority. A conforming pack declares its operational sources, maps them through accepted Neutral Finance interfaces, proves one effect bundle per source, supplies deterministic economic control totals, preserves scope and authorization, and demonstrates safe replay and recovery.

The generic contract and evaluator contain no WND or restaurant semantics. The frozen M7 WND/Restaurant support is the first specimen profile. It covers commercial sale (including discount, complimentary treatment and receivable creation), collection, receivable repayment, refund/reversal, verified inventory COGS and financial-document handoff. M7's side-effect-free mapping and shadow evidence remain unchanged.

Hidden-writer proof combines an AST-based scan of pack-facing implementation string literals with disposable-database execution through the canonical event engine. The runtime probe proves one accepted fact, stable replay, fail-closed conflicting duplicate, cross-organization rejection and rollback without residue. Economic source expectations are compared with canonical control totals; balanced journals alone are not sufficient evidence.

The readiness report is evidence for R6 review. It always keeps `writer_routing=unchanged`, `cutover=NOT_AUTHORIZED`, `writer_retirement=NOT_EXECUTED`, `migration=NONE`, and the canonical head `m64_reconciliation_controls_020`. M8.4 does not mutate production data, change M7, or perform live cutover.
