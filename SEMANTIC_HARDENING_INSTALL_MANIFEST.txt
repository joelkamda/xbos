Revision 3 correction
---------------------
Revision 2 exposed the next PostgreSQL acceptance defect: SC41 timestamped operations
were converted to frozen PC3 DATE-effective semantic-version dates by PostgreSQL
CAST(timestamptz AS date). That conversion depends on the database session timezone and
can resolve midnight UTC as the previous calendar day. Revision 3 derives the PC3
semantic date explicitly in UTC before binding. The same bridge is used for concept
resolution, classification snapshots, hierarchy semantic-version selection, and graph
mapping selection. No authority or schema semantics are broadened.
