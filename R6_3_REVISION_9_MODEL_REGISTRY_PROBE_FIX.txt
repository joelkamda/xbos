XBOS R6.3 WND APPLICATION COMPATIBILITY + UAT — REVISION 9
==========================================================

Observed Revision 8 acceptance failure:
  The R6.3 compatibility migration itself upgraded successfully to
  r63_legacy_inventory_writer_compat_044. The subsequent diagnostic ORM
  probe failed before issuing its inventory INSERT because SQLAlchemy could
  not resolve InventoryItem.atomic_unit -> 'AtomicUnit'.

Root cause:
  The isolated probe imported InventoryItem directly, unlike the real WND
  startup path. Frozen WND startup imports core.models_import first, which
  registers Tenant/Branch/User/RBAC/Taxonomy/AtomicUnit/Sales/Payments/
  Inventory/Accounting models in the shared SQLAlchemy registry.

Correction:
  - the isolated legacy-inventory probe now imports core.models_import before
    importing/querying InventoryItem and InventoryMovement;
  - a regression contract pins this exact startup-equivalent registry load.

The migration SQL and all business semantics are unchanged. Production
writes remain NONE; writer routing remains unchanged; live cutover remains
NOT AUTHORIZED.
